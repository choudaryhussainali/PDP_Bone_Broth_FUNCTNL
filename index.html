The Ritual

Post-workout, it delivers 19g of clean protein and 16.8g of collagen to support muscle repair and connective tissue recovery. First thing in the morning, the glutamine and collagen support gut lining health and set the foundation for the day. Before bed, glycine supports sleep quality while collagen synthesis peaks overnight.

Serving size: 1 scoop (20g). 15 servings per pouch.

Hot broth: Add one scoop to a mug or cup. Pour over 200–250ml of hot (not boiling) water. Mix thoroughly using a frother until fully dissolved and smooth. Season with turmeric, Himalayan pink salt, black pepper and spices to taste – we love to add lemon.

Smoothie: Add one scoop to a blender with your chosen liquid and ingredients. Blend thoroughly until fully dissolved. Do not shake — blending is essential for full dissolution.

Cooking: Stir into soups, stews, sauces or oats during preparation.

Best consumed post-workout for recovery, in the morning for gut health, or before bed to support overnight collagen synthesis.

Before you order

Questions, answered honestly.

The five things people ask most before their first pouch. If yours isn’t here, we reply to every message.

Ask us anything
01What does it actually taste like?

Like a clean, savoury broth — not a shake. It’s unflavoured and unsweetened, so on its own it is mild and slightly meaty. Most people season it: a pinch of pink salt, black pepper and turmeric, or a squeeze of lemon. Blended into a smoothie or stirred into soup, you don’t taste it at all.

02Why does it clump, and do I really need the frother?

Yes. Bone broth powder is mostly collagen, and collagen won’t dissolve by shaking. Ten seconds with the frother in hot (not boiling) water and it goes completely clear. That’s why the frother comes free with every subscription — skip it and you’ll get the clumpy result that turns into three-star reviews.

03Is it halal certified?

Every batch is halal certified, from Nordic grass-fed beef raised without hormones or antibiotics. The certification covers the whole product, not just the source, and the certificate is available on request.

04How is this different from whey?

Whey is a dairy isolate; this is one ingredient. A 20g scoop gives you 19g of protein and 17g of collagen with no lactose, no sweeteners and no flavouring — 84 calories in total. If whey leaves you bloated, the dairy is usually the reason, and there is none here.

05How does the subscription work?

Pick your pack, and it ships on the schedule you choose at 10% off the one-time price, with the frother included free. Skip a delivery, change your pack size or cancel from your account at any time — no minimum term, no phone call required.
