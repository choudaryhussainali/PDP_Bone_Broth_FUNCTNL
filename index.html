Before you buy

The questions
people actually ask.

What does it actually taste like?
Savoury. It's unflavoured beef bone broth, so it tastes like a light, clean beef stock — not a vanilla shake. Most people either drink it as a hot broth with turmeric, black pepper and salt, or blend it into a smoothie where it disappears entirely.
Why does it need a frother, and do I get one?
Bone broth protein is a fine, dense powder that won't fully dissolve from shaking — it will clump. Ten seconds with a handheld frother and it goes completely clear. Every subscription order includes the FUNCTNL frother free (worth £11.99); you can add one to a one-time order for £11.99.
How long does one pouch last?
300g gives 15 servings at one 20g scoop a day, so a single pouch is roughly two weeks. If you're taking it daily, the 2 Pack is a genuine month — which is why the subscription ships two pouches every 30 days rather than one.
Can I use it alongside whey, or instead of it?
Either. Plenty of customers replace whey entirely because it was what caused the problem. Others keep whey post-training and use this in the morning or before bed for the collagen and glycine.
Who certifies it as halal?
The beef is halal certified and sourced hormone- and antibiotic-free from Nordic grass-fed herds. [Insert certifying body and certificate number — naming the certifier is the whole point of the claim.]
Will it break a fast?
Yes. At 84 calories and 19g of protein a serving will break a fast in any meaningful sense. Many customers use it as the first thing they break a fast with, because it's low volume and easy on an empty stomach.
What if I don't get on with it?
Send it back. First-time customers can return opened pouches within 30 days for a full refund. Returning customers need product unopened. One refund per household. Full returns terms.
Pregnant, breastfeeding, or on medication?
Speak to your GP or a healthcare professional before starting any new supplement. This is a single-ingredient food product rather than a formulation, but that advice stands regardless.
